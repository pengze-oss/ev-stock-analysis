Rivian Significant Movement Analysis

Event window: January 2–10, 2025.

Rivian's closing price rose from $13.25 on January 2 to $16.49 on January 3, a daily return of approximately 24.45%. 
These were the lowest and highest closing prices in the selected window.

On January 3, Rivian reported 2024 deliveries of 51,579 vehicles, within its guidance of 50,500–52,000. 
It also announced that a component shortage was no longer limiting production. 
This news may have improved investors' expectations about operational performance.

Three data-backed findings:
1. January 3 had the largest positive daily return in the 2025 sample: 24.45%.
2. Trading volume was 124,581,800 shares, approximately 3.6 times the full-year median of 34,630,100 shares, 
indicating unusually active trading.
3. By January 10, the closing price had fallen to $13.85. The window's overall price increase was only 4.53%, 
so much of the initial gain had reversed.

The timing, return and volume are consistent with a positive market reaction to the announcement. 
However, they do not prove causation; other news and broader market movements may also have contributed.
